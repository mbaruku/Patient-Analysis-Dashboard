# Patient-Analysis-Dashboard
I developed a comprehensive project in powerbi, creating patient analysis dashboard.  This process involved several stages icluding data cleaning and data visualization 

*Key Insights*
1,000 total patients analyzed across four healthcare services.
Average patient age: 45 years.
Average length of stay: 7 days.
Average satisfaction score: 79.60.
Age distribution: Adults aged 18–64 represent 61.8% of patients.
Patient satisfaction: Surgery, ICU, and Emergency recorded scores of 80, while General Medicine scored 79.
Satisfaction ranges: 528 patients scored between 61 and 80, highlighting opportunities to improve patient experience.

Length of stay: Surgery and ICU recorded the highest average stay at 8 days.

Service utilization: Emergency handled the highest number of patients (263), followed by Surgery (254).

Monthly trends: Patient volumes fluctuate throughout the year, peaking around October before declining toward December.

**Recommendations**

Improve patient satisfaction through feedback analysis.

Optimize staffing and resource allocation across healthcare services.

Investigate factors contributing to longer hospital stays.

Monitor monthly patient trends to support operational planning.

Use data-driven insights to improve healthcare service delivery.
